Painting services

Every job starts with the same care for surface preparation, whether it's one wall or a whole building.

Putty and crack filling, primer, and two to three coats of emulsion — with corners and edges cut clean by hand.

Exterior Wall Painting

Weatherproof exterior emulsion over properly sealed cracks, with scaffolding used safely on multi-storey walls.

Waterproofing & Crack Treatment

How we paint a room

Five stages, every time — nothing skipped to save a day.

Site Visit & Colour Consultation

We check wall condition, moisture and old paint type, and help you choose shades and finishes that suit the room.

Surface Preparation

Flaking paint is scraped off, cracks and holes are filled with putty, and walls are sanded smooth.

Priming

A primer coat is applied so the final colour goes on even and lasts, especially over patched or new plaster.

Painting

Two to three coats of emulsion or exterior paint are rolled and brushed on, with drying time respected between coats.

Final Check & Cleanup

We inspect every wall in daylight, touch up thin spots, and clear away sheets, tape and tins before we leave.

Common questions

Quick answers before you call.

How long does painting a 2BHK home take?

A typical 2BHK, walls only, takes about 4 to 6 working days including drying time between coats. Exterior work and taller rooms can take longer.

Should I buy the paint, or do you supply it?

Either way works. Most clients ask us to buy from a brand and shade they've chosen, and we show the bill. If you already have paint at home, we're happy to use it.

Do you work on Sundays?

We keep Sundays for urgent or ongoing jobs only. Regular work is scheduled Monday to Saturday so the team gets a proper day off.

Is there a warranty on the paint job?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship for 12 months against peeling or flaking that isn't caused by water damage or structural cracks.

Ready to repaint or redesign a wall?

Tell us the rooms or walls involved and we'll visit to give you a firm estimate.
