How a project runs

The same five stages, whether it's one door or a full house.

Site Visit & Estimate

We inspect the walls, wood or ceiling in person, discuss what you want, and give you a written estimate before any work starts.

Surface Preparation

Old paint is scraped or sanded, cracks are filled with putty, and surfaces are cleaned and left to dry properly.

Primer & Base Coat

A suitable primer goes down first, sealing the surface and helping the final coats last longer.

Finish Work

Paint, polish or ceiling boards go on with proper drying time between stages, and edges are finished by hand.

Quality Check & Handover

We walk the site with you, touch up anything that needs it, clean up, and hand the space back.

Why families & shop owners choose us

The details that matter once the tins are open.

Proper Surface Preparation

We don't paint over problems. Cracks are filled, flaking paint is scraped back, and surfaces are primed before any finish coat goes on.

Trusted Materials

We work with Asian Paints, Berger, Nerolac and other established brands, and tell you exactly what's going on your walls before we buy it.

Skilled, Supervised Team

Every job is led by an experienced painter on site, not a trainee learning on your walls.

Safety on Site

Proper scaffolding, ladders tied off, and dust sheets over your furniture and floors — not a bedsheet balanced on two chairs.

On-Time Completion

We agree a finish date before starting, and tell you straight away if weather or site conditions change it.

Clean Handover

Floors swept, drop cloths folded, tins removed — the room looks like a room again before we ask for final payment.
