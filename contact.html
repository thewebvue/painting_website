Quick answers before you call.

Do you give a free site visit and estimate?

Yes, for jobs in and around Polur taluk. For sites further away we may ask for photos first and confirm a visit once the scope is clear.

Do you take small jobs, like a single room or one door?

Yes. Plenty of our work is a single room repaint or one wardrobe re-polished — we don't require a full-house project.

Which areas do you cover?

Our base is Kannigapuram, Polur, and day-to-day work is across Kannigapuram, Polur, Kalambur, Arani, Vandavasi, Cheyyar. For larger residential or commercial contracts, our 20-member team travels across India — tell us your location and project size and we will confirm.
